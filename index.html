Who We Are

A Legacy of Engineering Excellence

Valli Trading Limited was established in 2001 in Gibraltar with a clear mission: to finance and implement world-class investment and construction projects. Under the leadership of Chairman Mr Raymond Brown, the company has grown into a trusted name in infrastructure development across three continents.

Our operations span the United Kingdom, the Middle East, and Central Asia. We work closely with our associate companies — Alkanz Ltd in Iraq and Nationwide Technologies in the United Kingdom — each receiving strategic finance and direction from Valli Trading Limited.

Our track record includes the largest piling works in the entire Middle East, iconic bridges over the Tigris and Euphrates rivers, international-standard ports, power stations, and multi-lane motorways.

What We Do

Core Services

Bridge Construction

Design and construction of major river crossings, including iconic bridges across the Tigris and Euphrates — the largest bridges in Mesopotamia.

Piling Works

Over 100,000 precast concrete piles driven — the largest piling operation in the Middle East. Specialists in all pile types for infrastructure, industrial, and marine projects.

Power Stations

Engineering and construction of thermal power stations and electrical infrastructure across Iraq, Kuwait, and the wider region.

Ports & Marine

Construction of port facilities, marine retaining walls, and jetty piling, including works at Umm Qasr — Iraq's principal deep-water port.

Motorways & Roads

Delivery of high-speed motorways including the Baghdad–Abu Ghraib expressway, and seven bridge intersections — in partnership with leading international contractors.

Investment & Finance

Strategic financing and direction for infrastructure projects, channelling investment through our network of associate companies across the UK, Middle East, and Central Asia.

Our Network

Associate Companies

Valli Trading Limited directs and finances a network of associate companies, each a recognised leader in its local market.

Alkanz Ltd

🏛 Iraq (Baghdad)

Alkanz (Khairat Al-Kanz) is Iraq's premier precast concrete and piling company, founded in 2004 and ISO 9001-2015 certified. Operating from Iraq's first and largest precast piling factory in the Uwairej Industrial Area near Baghdad, Alkanz is known throughout Iraq as the preferred supplier of piles, fencing, and precast elements.

  • Precast concrete piling (up to 2,000 m/day capacity)
  • Sheet pile installation — ground & marine
  • Precast perimeter fencing & walls
  • Ready-mix concrete
  • General contracting
